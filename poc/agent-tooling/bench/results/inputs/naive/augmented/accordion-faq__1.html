Frequently Asked Questions

Click the "Sign up" button in the top-right corner, fill in your email address and a password, then press Enter to submit. You will receive a confirmation email to verify your account. Use the "Forgot password" link on the login form. We will email you a secure reset link that expires after 24 hours. Follow the link and choose a new password. We accept major credit and debit cards, as well as popular digital wallets. During checkout you will see the full list of supported payment methods for your region. Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery as long as the product is in its original condition. Contact support to start a return and get a prepaid shipping label.
