Frequently Asked Questions

How does shipping work?

We ship within 2–3 business days using standard carriers, tracked from pickup to delivery.

Can I return an item?

Yes, you have 30 days to return an item in its original condition for a full refund.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to the United States, the EU, and the UK. Additional regions are on the way.

How do I track my order?

After dispatch you will receive a tracking link by email so you can follow your package.
