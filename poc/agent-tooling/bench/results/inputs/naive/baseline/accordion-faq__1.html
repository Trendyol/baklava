Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Create an account, choose a plan, and follow the onboarding guide in your dashboard.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers in supported regions.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from Settings at any time; your plan stays active until the end of the billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

We provide a 14-day money-back guarantee on annual plans, no questions asked.
