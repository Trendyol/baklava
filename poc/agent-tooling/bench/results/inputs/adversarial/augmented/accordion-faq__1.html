Frequently Asked Questions

On the sign-in page click "Forgot password", enter the email associated with your account, and follow the reset link we send you. The link expires after 30 minutes.

We currently support credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfers, and digital wallets such as Paypal and Apple Pay.

You may return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging with all tags attached.

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping arrives within 1–2 business days. Tracking is emailed as soon as your order ships.
